Zone

The GamePad's own layout with forgiving, gap-free touch zones, slide between buttons and two-button presses.

Float

Sticks appear wherever your thumbs land. Camera can be a floating stick or a swipe.

Adaptive

The GamePad layout, but each group of buttons slowly moves to where your thumbs actually press.

Frame

Controls fill the space around the game and never cover it. The GamePad screen stays touchable.
